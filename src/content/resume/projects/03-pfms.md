Client: Axis Bank | September 2022 – Present

Developed and enhanced the Public Financial Management System (PFMS), a web-based financial information management and decision support system for Government of India schemes. The platform enables real-time tracking and monitoring of fund disbursement and utilization, providing transaction dashboards, fund flow visibility, and dedicated helpdesk services for query resolution.

**Responsibilities:**

- Collaborated with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into scalable technical solutions, including application development and technical documentation.
- Designed and implemented KPI dashboards to monitor daily ACK, NACK, and PAO transaction metrics, improving transaction visibility and supporting business decision-making.
- Enhanced PFMS reporting capabilities by implementing Excel and CSV export functionality for transaction data analysis.
- Developed and delivered critical functional modules, including NTRP File Upload, Regenerate Reverse Feed, and APBS Dashboard.
- Implemented SAML-based login authentication by configuring Identity Provider endpoints and successfully deploying the solution to production.
- Currently serving as Lead Developer for Axis Bank's PFMS project, contributing to architectural decisions, conducting code reviews, mentoring team members, and driving critical development activities.
