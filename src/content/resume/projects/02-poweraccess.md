Client: Axis Bank | September 2022 – Present

Developed and enhanced Power Access, a Host-to-Host (H2H) integrated payment solution for corporate customers, enabling seamless business payment processing through direct ERP integration. The platform supports multiple payment methods, including NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, Demand Draft, and Customer Cheque, providing end-to-end control over payment execution and reconciliation.

**Responsibilities:**

- Participated in requirement gathering, backend development, and technical documentation, collaborating with stakeholders to deliver business-aligned solutions.
- Implemented encryption and decryption mechanisms for sensitive data and integrated masking of financial information, including account numbers and IFSC codes, in application logs to ensure data security and compliance.
- Optimized database stored procedures for bulk data processing, achieving a 50% reduction in execution time.
- Designed and developed a user interface to display SFTP file listings and implemented file management functionalities, including move, rename, and delete operations.
- Contributed to migrating a legacy Spring MVC application to Spring Boot, improving application modularity, configuration management, and deployment efficiency.
- Took ownership of critical development activities in the absence of senior developers, ensuring smooth project execution and timely delivery.
